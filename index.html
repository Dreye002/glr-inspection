Straight answers

Inspection FAQ

The questions homeowners ask most.

Is it really free? What's the catch?+

There isn't one. The roof inspection and photo report are genuinely free, with no obligation to hire us. We get on your roof, document what we see, and send you the full photo report to keep, even if you end up choosing another roofer. If we don't deliver your report within 24–48 hours of your inspection, you get a $100 credit toward any future repair.

How long does it take?+

Most inspections take around 30 to 45 minutes, depending on the roof.

Do I need to be home?+

It helps so we can walk you through what we find, but it is not always required. We will confirm when we schedule.

Will you go in the attic?+

When access is available and you are comfortable with it, checking from underneath gives a fuller picture. We always ask first.

Do you inspect active leaks?+

Yes. If you have an active leak, tell us so we can prioritize you.

What happens after the inspection?+

You get your full photo report within 24-48 hours, and it's yours to keep. If repairs or replacement make sense, we explain your options clearly, repair vs. replace, so you can decide on your own timeline. No surprises, no hard sell.

Can I compare another quote?+

Absolutely. We are happy to help you compare scope and options so you can decide with confidence.

Can you just give me a price over the phone?+

For existing roofs, no. Any price without seeing your actual roof is just a guess, and we don't guess with your home. We start with a free Photo Proof Roof Inspection, Report & Estimate so your number is based on real photos and measurements.

For brand-new builds or major additions with plans, we can often give an estimate from your plans without an inspection.

Do you offer financing?+

Financing options may be available for qualified homeowners. Your project team can explain what applies to your project.
